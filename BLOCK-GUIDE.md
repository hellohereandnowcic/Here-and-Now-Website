# Editing the site: your building blocks

Every page on the site (The Bulletin, V is for Victims, Archive, About, Team, Press, Support, News) is built from a list of **blocks**. In the CMS admin panel, open a page and you'll see its blocks listed top to bottom — that list is the whole page, in order.

You can:

- **Edit** any block by clicking into it and changing its text, image, or settings.
- **Reorder** blocks by dragging them up or down — this changes the order things appear on the page.
- **Add** a new block with the "+ Add" button, then pick a type from the list below.
- **Delete** a block you no longer want.

Nothing here touches code. Every change is just filling in a form.

## The 9 block types

**Hero** — the big title banner at the top of a page. Fields: title, a short meta line underneath (e.g. "New musical comedy — 2027"), and whether it sits on a dark background. Every page should start with one of these.

**Text + Image** — a two-column section: a photo on one side, a heading and paragraph(s) on the other. You choose whether the image sits left or right, and pick a background colour from the list (paper, white, one of the show colours, etc.). Good for "What it is," bios, or any storytelling section paired with a photo.

**Statement** — a big standalone quote or pull-line, centered, no image. Good for a mission statement or a strong one-liner. Has its own background colour setting.

**Text** — just a heading and paragraph(s), no image. Use this for anything text-only: an honest note, a short explainer, contact details.

**Gallery** — a grid of photos with captions. Use for "Looking Back" / archive-style photo sections.

**Credits** — the colour-coded list of names and roles (like "Guest Host," "Composer," "Book & Lyrics"). Add one row per person; each row can have its own colour, or it'll cycle through the show's colours automatically.

**FAQ** — a collapsible question-and-answer accordion. Add as many question/answer pairs as you like.

**News list** — a simple list of dated announcements, each with a headline and body text. Used on the News page.

**Newsletter** — the "join the list" sign-up prompt with a heading and subtext. Most pages end with one of these.

## A few notes

- Colour names you can type into any "background" or "text colour" field: `paper`, `white`, `accent`, `ink`, plus each show's own colours (`bulletin`, `bulletin-a`, `bulletin-b`, `victims`, `victims-a`, `victims-b`, `archive`, `archive-a`, `archive-b`). Typing one of these keeps everything on-brand; you can also type a raw colour code if you ever need something different.
- Text automatically switches to white or dark depending on how light or dark the background you pick is — you don't need to set that yourself.
- The homepage tiles (Bulletin / V is for Victims / Archive) are edited separately, under "Homepage" in the CMS — that's just three photos and two caption lines each, not a block list.
- If a page looks broken after an edit, the most common cause is an empty required field (like a hero with no title). Check the block you just changed first.
