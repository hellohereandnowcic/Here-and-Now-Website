What we do

We make theatre for the here and now, which for us means theatre and performance where the relationship between the actor and the audience is the most important thing. The space doesn't need to be a conventional one — in fact, we've transformed a lot of spaces that weren't meant to be theatres into performance arenas. We think this results in an even more live and electric experience for everyone in the room.

Archive — The Shipwright

2020–2026 · Deptford

In 2020 we took up residence in the Master Shipwright's House, a 500-year-old building on the Thames in Deptford, and founded The Shipwright — a theatre and music venue where the company lived, ate, and made work together, two seasons a year: an outdoor summer spectacular and an intimate indoor winter festival. Over six years it became a headline venue for Lewisham's year as the Mayor of London's Borough of Culture, and made work the Guardian called “exquisite, ambitious and inquisitive.”

As the house moves into new ownership, that chapter is ending. We're starting a new one — The Bulletin, V is for Victims, and The Peckham Public Theatre, our co-production with Peckham Festival.

Who we are

Joseph Winters

Joseph is a director and producer of theatre and arts based in London, and founder of The Shipwright. He was Resident Director at the Almeida Theatre (2018–2019) and Staff Director of English Touring Opera, and in 2023 became Artistic Director of Peckham Levels. He has worked alongside Michael Longhurst, Natalie Abrahami, Rupert Goold and Robert Icke, and has taught at Oxford, Central Saint Martins, Goldsmiths and Rose Bruford College.

Emma Halstead

Emma is a producer who has worked across theatre, dance and film in the UK and internationally, including with the Donmar Warehouse, the Barbican, Sadler's Wells, the London Palladium, Birmingham Rep and St Ann's Warehouse in New York. A Stage One London Producer graduate, she joined as Executive Director of The Shipwright, building the formal structure behind the company's work.
